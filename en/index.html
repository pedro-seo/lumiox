Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about
home automation in Barcelona

How much does it cost to install home automation in an apartment in Barcelona?+
The price of a home automation installation varies depending on the size of the home, the scope of the project, and the desired features. We work with both wireless systems that require no construction work and fully wired installations such as KNX, adapting with high flexibility to each client's real needs. At Lumiox, we offer a free, personalized quote so you know the exact cost of your project.
Can home automation be installed without construction work?+
Yes, absolutely. There are wireless home automation systems (such as Zigbee, Z-Wave, or Wi-Fi) that can be installed without breaking through walls or carrying out renovations. They are ideal for rental apartments or homes where you don't want construction work. Our installers in Barcelona specialize in both approaches: with construction work for new builds or full renovations, and without construction work for already-occupied homes.
What home automation systems do you install in Barcelona?+
We work with the leading standards and manufacturers on the market: KNX (the European standard par excellence), Loxone (ideal for single-family homes), Control4 and Crestron (for high-end projects), and wireless solutions such as Zigbee and Z-Wave. All our systems are compatible with Alexa, Google Assistant, and Apple HomeKit.
How much can you save on your electricity bill with home automation?+
A well-configured home automation system can reduce energy consumption by between 25% and 40%. This is achieved through smart lighting management (automatic shutoff, dimming based on natural light), scheduled climate control, and standby control for appliances. The final savings on your electricity bill depend on the size of the home and each household's consumption habits.
How long does a home automation installation take?+
It depends entirely on the scope of the project: a basic wireless installation is very different from a complete wired KNX system or a single-family home and larger commercial projects. At Lumiox, we always give you a clear, realistic timeline before starting, tailored to the specific needs of your home or business.
Does home automation still work if the internet goes down?+
Yes. The professional systems we install (KNX, Loxone) operate locally, without depending on the cloud or the internet. Automations, scenes, and controls remain operational even if the connection drops. Internet is only needed for remote control from outside the home and for integrations with voice assistants.
