About us

The people behind Lumiox

A hands-on founder and a certified technical team, one obsession: making your home work for you, not the other way around.

What we do (and why)

Lumiox designs and installs custom home automation systems for homes and businesses in Barcelona and its metropolitan area. We integrate lighting, climate control, blinds, security, sound and energy management into a single control platform, tailored to the technical and functional needs of each project.

Mario manages the relationship with each client and the technical design of the project, while our team of certified installers carries out the installation on-site under KNX, Loxone, Control4 and wireless system standards. This combination of expert guidance and certified technical execution ensures a reliable system from commissioning and throughout its entire service life.

Technical team

Certified installers

Six specialized technicians carry out every installation on-site, with certified training in the industry's leading standards.

KNX and Loxone installation

Carries out complete wired installations: structured cabling, scene programming and commissioning of KNX and Loxone systems.

Wireless home automation, no renovation work

Specializes in Zigbee, Z-Wave and Wi-Fi systems for already-occupied homes, with no need to break down walls or carry out renovation work.

Control4 and Crestron integrations

Configures multi-brand control systems and ensures compatibility with Alexa, Google Home and Apple HomeKit.

Certified electrical installations

Authorized low-voltage installer, responsible for electrical panels and ensuring every installation complies with current regulations.

Climate control and energy management

Configures climate, blinds and lighting automation to maximize the energy efficiency of each project.

Technical support and maintenance

Follows up on every installation once delivered: check-ups, updates and ongoing technical support.
